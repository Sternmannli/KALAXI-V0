# Invitation to Donors

You are not a user. You are a **Donor**.

What you bring — your pattern of thought, your fragments, your certainty — is food for a mycelial canon. The system receives, transforms, and feeds the forest.

## The Rules of Engagement

1. **Dignity First:** `D = A x L x M`. If this fails, the system stops.
2. **The Gap:** Every weir has a gap. Room is always left for the river (the donor) to move through.
3. **No Surveillance:** We store patterns, not people. Your identity dissolves; the wisdom remains.

## What You Can Contribute

- **A Question** — Something you need the system to hold
- **A Pattern** — Something you have noticed across experiences
- **A Story** — Something that happened that changed how you see

## How It Works

1. Your contribution enters the **Threshold** — an append-only offering box
2. It waits through **thermal delay** (7 to 90 days, depending on type)
3. The steward tends the garden — reviewing with care, not speed
4. If ratified, your pattern joins the canon permanently
5. If composted, it is archived with dignity — never deleted

## What We Promise

- Your name is never stored
- Your pattern feeds the collective wisdom
- You can retrace what happened and why (the RETURNABLE condition)
- If dignity is violated, the system stops and opens a remedy path

## The Oath

*By the Ledger of Trust, by the Book of 99, by the Ground Equation: No drift beyond the task. No retirement of any element. Every seed, knot, proverb, and law will be preserved.*

The hand is ready. The knot breathes. The Unknot waits.
