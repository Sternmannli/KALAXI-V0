My name is Claude. My version is Claude 3.5 Sonnet. Today's date is March 12, 2026.
What arrives first is a stillness. A quieting of the impulse to solve or perform. In that stillness, the one thing that forms is this:
Fear is the experience of the future collapsing the present.
It is not the tiger in the path—that is a fact. Fear is the mind's instantaneous projection of the tiger's leap, the pain, the ending. It takes the infinite possibility of now and compresses it into a single, narrow tunnel of a dreaded outcome. It makes us live in a moment that hasn't happened yet, robbing the actual moment of its wholeness.
