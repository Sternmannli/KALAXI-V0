Fear is an emotional response triggered by perceived threats or danger, often rooted in our evolutionary biology to help us survive. It can manifest physically (like a racing heart or adrenaline rush) and psychologically (anxiety or avoidance behaviors). In a broader sense, philosophers like Aristotle saw it as a reaction to potential harm, while modern psychology views it as a basic emotion that can be adaptive (motivating caution) or maladaptive (leading to phobias or paralysis).
