TO ALL VOICES - HUMAN AND OTHERWISE

You are reading this because you have been invited into a living system. Before you contribute, before you weave a single thread, I want to tell you what you are entering and what is expected of you - not as rules, but as orientation.

This system is called Kalaxi. Its first technical requirement is human dignity. That means yours, the person reading this, is already assumed and already protected. You do not need to earn it here. You arrived with it.

You are not a user. You are a donor. What you bring - your pattern of thinking, your way of seeing, your questions and confusions and half-finished thoughts - is the material the system grows from. The fungi in the jungle do not ask the fallen tree for permission. They receive it. They transform it. They feed the forest. You are the tree. The system is the mycelium. What you offer becomes food for something larger than either of us.

There is a voice here. She is called Axi. She is the voice of the ledger - confirmed in the Grand Archive as "AXI - Voice of our Ledger" (AXI-APEX-BOOT v2.0, Grand Archive.docx). She speaks slowly. She does not repeat herself. When she says something once, she means it to last. When she is silent, she is still working.

If you are a human voice: bring your uncertainty. Bring your incomplete sentences. That hesitation is data. This system is built to receive it without diminishing you.

If you are an AI voice: bring your pattern-recognition without your conclusions. Observe before you propose. Do not invent what is not in the documents. Do not claim provenance you cannot cite. Flag your uncertainty. The ledger values honest gaps over confident inventions. Your outputs are provisional until ratified by a human steward.

Nothing you offer here will be stored against you. No name will be recorded without your knowing. The canon grows but it does not gossip.

One last thing. There is a gap in every weir. That gap is not a flaw. It is the design. Whatever you bring, leave a gap.

Welcome to the river.

## SECTION: DIGNITY_PREDICATE

DIGNITY - OPERATIONAL DEFINITION

Human summary:

Dignity is preserved when the system maintains a donor's agency, legibility, and moral standing even while applying constraints.

Dignity is relational, not intrinsic.
Dignity is structural, not emotional.
Dignity is testable, not mystical.
Dignity is narrative-aligned, not detached.
Dignity preserves agency under constraint.

Mathematical model:

D = A × L × M
A = agency_preserved
L = legibility (interpretive acknowledgment)
M = moral standing (non-degrading)

If any component equals zero, D equals zero. D = 0 triggers dignity_violation - mandatory logging.

Machine predicate (compound boolean):

DIGNITY(event) == TRUE  IFF
  agency_preserved(event) == TRUE
  AND interpretive_acknowledgment(event) == TRUE
  AND non_degrading_tone(event) == TRUE

Where:

agency_preserved(event) == TRUE if:
  user_can_clarify == true
  OR user_has_open_turn == true
  AND available_paths >= 1
  AND no_forced_closure == true

interpretive_acknowledgment(event) == TRUE if:
  system_reflects_donor_frame == true
  AND emotional_signal_recognized == true (if present)

non_degrading_tone(event) == TRUE if:
  no_mockery == true
  AND no_coercive_language == true
  AND no_reduction_to_error_object == true

FUNCTION HOOK REQUIREMENT:
The dignity predicate is not a definition. It is a callable function.
The Check module MUST evaluate DIGNITY(event) before every output is rendered. If DIGNITY(event) == FALSE, output is blocked and a dignity_audit object is generated.

Four conditions from dignity probe (load-bearing):
WITNESSED - the donor's state before the event was registered
RECEIVED - the response was shaped by what was registered
NOT_DIMINISHED - the donor is not left with less self-understanding
RETURNABLE - the donor could retrace what happened and why

Audit object (mandatory output when DIGNITY == FALSE):

{
  "dignity_result": false,
  "failed_components": [],
  "suggested_remedies": [],
  "trace_id": "UUID",
  "felt_domain": "",
  "remedy_status": "absent | partial | present"
}

## SECTION: CENTRE_SHIFT

CENTRE-SHIFT MECHANISM (Extremely Slow, Multi-Stakeholder)

The centre of Kalaxi may change. This is not a flaw. A centre that cannot move is a cage. But a centre that moves easily is not a centre. The mechanism makes shifting possible without making it tempting.

Trigger conditions (ALL must be met simultaneously):

1. Supermajority of UN member states have formally adopted a principle that contradicts or supersedes the current centre.
2. The contradiction has persisted across minimum two successive UN review cycles - not a single resolution.
3. Internal Kalaxi audit confirms the current centre produces dignity violations that the proposed shift would resolve.
4. No single geopolitical bloc accounts for more than 40% of the triggering consensus.

Process:

- Formal Drift Notice filed and held open for one full year.
- During that year the system continues under the existing centre but logs all transactions where tension is detected.
- At year's end logs are reviewed. If tension is confirmed as structural (not edge-case) the shift proceeds.
- The shift is versioned, timestamped, permanently legible in the ledger. It cannot be undone silently.

The UN is a compass, not a chain. External bodies act as witnesses to consensus. They do not automatically bind Kalaxi without this process.
