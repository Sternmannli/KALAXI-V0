# Future Seeds — Latent Possibilities

Twelve ratified seeds — structurally latent in the architecture. Each awaits a steward decision before it can grow. They are not commitments; they are possibilities.

## The Twelve Seeds

1. **Distributed Stewardship** — Mechanism for anyone to become steward of their own personal or communal canon, federated but not centralized.

2. **Immutable Witness Network** — Distributed ledger of dignity events usable as legal and social evidence; Echo Stones as global witness nodes.

3. **Deliberative Democracy Infrastructure** — General-purpose slow-governance for any decision-making body; adaptive delay based on decision significance.

4. **Constitutional Evolution Engine** — Auto-detection of dignity failure clusters leading to proposed new covenants and amendments.

5. **Restorative Justice Platform** — Public space for dignity violation records; community witnessing; restorative processes.

6. **System Self-Awareness** — Meta-layer: a Wisdom Mirror of the System showing its own evolution, blind spots, and patterns.

7. **Personalized Parables** — Auto-generated mini-narratives for each anomaly, helping donors see their experience reflected in the canon.

8. **Institutional Dignity Score** — Audit tool: organization policies scored for dignity based on UDHR alignment.

9. **Negative Space Index** — Systematic detection of what the system consistently fails to metabolize; blind-spot measurement.

10. **Dignity Drift Detector** — Longitudinal analysis of dignity violation trends over time by domain. *(Already growing — operational as WEAVER/dignity_drift.py since 2026-03-08)*

11. **Proverb Stress Test Engine** — Continuous re-application of proverbs to new anomalies; automatic flagging of failing wisdom. *(Growing — planted 2026-03-13, WEAVER/proverb_stress_test.py, ready 2026-03-27)*

12. **Agency Amplifier** — Multidimensional agency measurement: visibility, affordability, accessibility, understandability of recourse. *(Growing — planted 2026-03-13, WEAVER/agency_amplifier.py, ready 2026-03-20)*
