The Dignity Predicate

D = A × L × M

A Agency

Can the person clarify, redirect, or stop?

L Legibility

Does the system reflect the person's actual context?

M Moral Standing

Is the person treated as a person?

  • Non-compensatory: high A cannot compensate for zero L
  • Multiplicative: any zero kills the product
  • Pre-response: computed before any output
  • The halt is the product: D=0 is evidence, not failure
