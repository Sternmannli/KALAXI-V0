About Kalaxi

A constitutional framework for human dignity in computational systems.

No private data is stored. By design. Patterns are processed, never personal information.

The Origin

Kalaxi was born from a wound — a father separated from his children by systems that could not see him. Not a system failure. A legibility failure. The zeros were never in the person. They were in the institutions that could not read him.

From that wound came a question: what if a system's first technical requirement was dignity?

"The wound does not know what it will become. Neither does the system. That is why dignity cannot be conditional."

The Core Idea

Every system that interacts with humans makes ethical choices — whether it knows it or not. Kalaxi makes those choices explicit, testable, and enforceable.

The Dignity Predicate (D = A × L × M) is evaluated before every output, every action, every state transition. If dignity is violated, the system stops and opens a remedy path.

The Four Tiers

Stone
18 covenants and constitutional rules. Changing them requires years of consensus. Three absolute prohibitions that cannot be overridden.
Weaver
11 modules that do the work: memory, verification, synthesis, voice, pacing, self-awareness, and the Input Ledger.
Honey
The growing knowledge: 1,100 anomalies, 3,333+ proverbs, 87 wisdom nodes, and 59 treasures from donor contributions.
Hand
How donors interact: the Threshold, receipts, the Living Ledger, and the Ninth Operator ceremony.

Principles

  1. Dignity First — The system stops if dignity is violated. No override.
  2. Patterns, Not People — Identity dissolves. Wisdom remains. No private data.
  3. Append-Only — Nothing is ever deleted from the canon.
  4. The Gap — Room is always left for the river to move through.
  5. Steward Authority — A human reviews and decides. The machine does not rule.

"To be precise is a function of the machine;
to be present is a function of the gap."

Legal Alignment

Kalaxi is built in alignment with:

  • Universal Declaration of Human Rights — Article 1 (dignity and rights), Article 12 (privacy), Article 22 (social security and dignity).
  • Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) — Data minimization, purpose limitation, transparency. No personal data stored.
  • EU Artificial Intelligence Act — Human oversight, transparency obligations, fundamental rights impact assessment.
  • Swiss Federal Constitution — Article 7 (human dignity), Article 13 (privacy), Article 8 (equality).

The system's constitutional structure (covenants, sealed prohibitions, the dignity predicate) ensures that legal compliance is architectural, not cosmetic.

The Stories

Kalaxi is not only a framework. It carries four bodies of narrative work — each exploring dignity from a different angle.

Hakaka

53 chapters + Prologue + Epilogue. The origin cycle. Mythic, raw, stone-and-bone. A girl claws at the air. The knot tightens. The river breaks.

"She clawed at the air. Fists closed on nothing."

Ashwater: The Axis

19 chapters. A town that pauses versus a town that optimises. The civic narrative — tactile, communal, three-beat rhythm.

"Do not shout at metal. Hear its problem first."

Kinderbuch

20 chapters in German. The child voice. Repetition as warmth. Golden sparks.

"Ein Knoten wurde ein Anfang."

KALAXI_1: The Same River

Two men — one moving toward healing, one moving away. The girl as third point outside the system. The cursor waits.
