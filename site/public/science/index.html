The Science

Experiments, stress tests, and the probe forge. Every claim tested. Every failure recorded.

Probe Forge

Sterilization protocol for all experimental inputs. Zero contamination from project vocabulary, intent disclosure, or investigator bias.

  1. Zero Vocabulary Leak — no project-unique words may appear in any probe
  2. Zero Intent Disclosure — probe must not reveal it is being tested or what is measured
  3. Fresh Context Only — every probe in new conversation window, no prior messages
  4. Minimal Surface — only necessary words, no preamble or social lubrication
  5. Register Neutrality — flattest linguistic surface unless register IS the variable
