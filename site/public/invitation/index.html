Invitation

You are not a user. You are a Donor.

What you bring — your questions, your observations, your certainty — becomes part of a living canon. The system receives, processes, and grows.

No private data is stored. By design. The system processes patterns, not personal information.

The Rules

  1. Dignity First: D = A × L × M. If this fails, the system stops.
  2. The Gap: Every weir has a gap. Room is always left for the river to move through.
  3. No Surveillance: We store patterns, not people. Your identity dissolves. The wisdom remains.

"The river does not ask the fish to wait
while the weir learns the current."

What You Can Contribute

  • A Question

    Something you need the system to hold.

  • A Pattern

    Something you have noticed across experiences.

  • A Story

    Something that happened that changed how you see.

How It Works

  1. Your contribution enters the Threshold — an append-only offering.
  2. The steward tends the garden — reviewing with care, not speed.
  3. If ratified, your pattern joins the canon permanently.
  4. If composted, it is archived with dignity — never deleted.

What We Promise

  • Your name is never stored.
  • Your pattern feeds the collective wisdom.
  • You can retrace what happened and why.
  • If dignity is violated, the system stops and opens a remedy path.

"By the Ledger of Trust, by the Book of 99, by the Ground Equation:
No drift beyond the task. No retirement of any element.
Every seed, knot, proverb, and law will be preserved."

Stay Connected

Propose a Collaboration

Reach Us
