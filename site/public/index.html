A Agency — Can the person clarify, redirect, or stop?

L Legibility — Does the system reflect the person's actual context?

M Moral Standing — Is the person treated as a person?

If any dimension reaches zero, the system stops. Non-compensatory. No override.

No private data is stored. By design.

The system processes anonymized contributions, not personal information. Your identity is not recorded. You have the right to request deletion at any time under FADP and GDPR.

You are not a user. You are a donor.

Ashwater is one of four narrative works in the kalam canon. It tells the story of two towns — one that pauses to listen, one that optimises without hearing. 19 chapters exploring civic dignity, communal decision-making, and what happens when systems stop listening to people.
