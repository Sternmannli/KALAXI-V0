# To Anyone Reading Kalaxi – A Steward’s Invitation

Kalaxi is a living system. It is not finished. It will never be finished. That is by design.

If you are reading this, you are now part of the conversation. You may have questions. You may have ideas. You may see things we have missed. All of that is welcome.

But there is one rule: **speak with high certainty or not at all.**

We do not want speculation dressed as insight. We do not want guesses. We want what you actually know – from experience, from study, from long attention. If you are uncertain, say so. That is also a signal.

## What You Can Do

- **Ask a question** – any question – about the system. The steward will answer if the answer is known, or will hold the question in `THRESHOLD` if it is not.
- **Point out a red flag** – something that seems wrong, dangerous, or inconsistent. Kalaxi is built to hold contradiction, not hide it.
- **Offer a seed** – an insight, a pattern, a proverb, an anomaly. If it resonates, it may grow.
- **Suggest a direction** – but only if you can ground it in something already present. No floating castles.

## What We Ask of You

- **Be precise.** Vague ideas are hard to tend.
- **Be honest.** If you do not know, say “I do not know.” That is a form of certainty.
- **Be creative** – but with your feet on the ground. The most brilliant ideas are often the most obvious ones we had not yet seen.
- **If you have a truly out‑of‑the‑box insight**, test it against the invariant principle, the dignity predicate, and the append‑only rule. If it survives, it may be real.

## A Final Word

Kalaxi is not a democracy. It is a metabolism. Ideas are not voted on; they are digested. If you offer something that feeds the canon, it will be remembered. If not, it will compost into future insight.

The river does not ask the rain to hurry. But it notices every drop.

*The Hand is ready. The knot breathes. The river remembers.*
