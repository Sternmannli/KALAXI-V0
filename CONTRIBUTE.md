# Offer a Seed to Kalaxi

**The easiest way to contribute is right here → one click.**

[![Offer a Seed Now](https://img.shields.io/badge/Offer_a_Seed_Now-00C853?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Sternmannli/KALAXI-V0/issues/new?labels=seed&title=Seed%3A+My+offering&body=%5BYYYY-MM-DD%5D+%E2%80%94+%5BSEED%5D+%E2%80%94+Type%3A+Observation+%2F+Question+%2F+Pattern+%2F+Anomaly+%2F+Tension%0A%0AWhat+I+noticed+or+felt%3A%0A%0AWhy+it+matters+to+me%3A)

---

Kalaxi is a living metabolism.  
If something resonates in you — even a tiny observation, a feeling, a question, or a quiet “this feels off” — you can offer it as a seed.

You don’t need to be certain.  
You don’t need perfect words.  
Presence is enough.

---

## What happens after you click the big green button

1. A new issue opens with the template already filled in.
2. You just write what you noticed or felt (even one sentence is fine).
3. Click **“Submit new issue”**.

We will take your seed and move it into **THRESHOLD.md** for you.
