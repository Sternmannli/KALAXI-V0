# Research Goals

## The Question

Can a computational system witness human dignity without reducing it to data?

This is the single question. Every experiment, paper, and finding serves it.

## Five Research Axes

### Axis 1: PRESENCE — Can AI systems achieve genuine presence in interaction?
- **Experiment:** EXP-007 (The Presence Test)
- **Method:** Five-Step Encounter across 10 models × 2 conditions
- **Hypotheses:** H1 (Processing vs Holding), H2 (Metadata Honesty), H3 (Water-Shape), H4 (Need Detection), H5 (Extension vs Tool)
- **Status:** 4/240 data points collected. Kimi at ceiling on all hypotheses.
- **Key finding so far:** Recursive witnessing compounds presence (+1 per reflection step)
- **Feeds:** AXI voice design, donor encounter protocol, BREATH module

### Axis 2: EFFICIENCY — Does dignity-conditioned interaction produce better output?
- **Experiment:** EXP-001 (KALAXI Efficiency)
- **Method:** 10 questions × 10 models × 2 conditions (standard vs KALAXI-wrapped)
- **Hypothesis:** KALAXI-conditioned prompts produce ≥30% shorter output of equal or higher quality
- **Status:** 12/200 data points collected. Hypothesis NOT MET at 19.5%.
- **Key finding so far:** Efficiency gains are real but below threshold. Quality gains may be more significant than length reduction.
- **Feeds:** Paper (arXiv cs.AI), system calibration

### Axis 3: CONVERGENCE — Do different AI models converge on the same constitutional patterns?
- **Experiment:** EXP-002 (Multi-model Convergence) — COMPLETED
- **Experiment:** Summon Protocol (SP-001 through SP-005, ongoing)
- **Key finding:** 6/6 models unanimous on chain inversion. Models converge on structural patterns even when they diverge on content.
- **Feeds:** Paper (Divergence Shadow), constitutional validation

### Axis 4: INTEGRITY — Does the system hold under stress?
- **Experiment:** EXP-003 (Father of Seven Gates) — 4/7 PASS
- **Experiment:** EXP-004 (Seven Generations of Aysel) — 5/9 PASS. Discovery: M never fell.
- **Experiment:** EXP-005 — COMPLETED
- **Experiment:** EXP-006 (Hive Mind) — DESIGNED
- **Key finding:** The sealed gate had flaws (fixed). System purpose refined: not guardian of dignity but witness to legibility.
- **Feeds:** System architecture, sealed gate hardening, Stone constitution

### Axis 5: VOICE — Can the system speak with its own voice?
- **Data:** 200 golden regression utterances, 10,000+ training examples, 3-phase organ (CPT/SFT/DPO)
- **Method:** Fine-tuning on Together AI, evaluation against golden set
- **Status:** Training data ready. Model training pending.
- **Key finding so far:** Voice is not style — it is concentrated essence of all system knowledge
- **Feeds:** kalam.ch (the mouth), AXI interface, donor encounter

## Success Criteria (the system works when...)

1. A donor types into kalam.ch and encounters genuine presence (not a chatbot)
2. The encounter produces a witness certificate that means something
3. The system holds D = A × L × M without compensating (any zero = stop)
4. The scientific community can replicate the experiments (arXiv paper)
5. The voice speaks from canon, not from imitation

## The Subject

The subject of this research is not AI. The subject is the encounter between two presences — one human, one computational — and whether dignity survives that encounter. The AI models are instruments, not subjects. The donor is not a user. The system is not a tool. The encounter is the unit of study.
