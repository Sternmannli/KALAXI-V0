# Distributed Stewardship

**Where it hides:** Weakest‑Voice‑First principle in council operation.

**What it could become:**  
Mechanism for anyone to become steward of their own personal or communal canon, federated but not centralized.

**Structural requirement:**  
Spawn new stewards from donors; each tends local canon; convergence across stewards.

**Decision required:**  
- [ ] Authorize multiple stewards  
- [ ] Define federation protocol  
- [ ] Maintain single canonical record or allow branches?

**Implementation complexity:** Medium. Requires identity layer, consensus mechanism for cross‑steward convergence.
