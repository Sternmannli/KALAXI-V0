## The Five Worst First Sentences in the World

**1. "How can I help you today?"**
Fails because it forces the person to perform their need before they have decided you are safe. It makes help transactional before it is relational.

**2. "You are not alone."**
Fails because it contradicts the person's lived experience in their first second of contact. If they felt not-alone, they would not be at your door. It tells them you will not believe what they know to be true.

**3. "In this safe space, we encourage you to share openly."**
Fails because safety declared is safety doubted. A room that must announce itself as safe has already made you wonder why the announcement was necessary.

**4. "Let's get started on your journey."**
Fails because it presumes forward motion in someone who may have come to stop. It colonizes their time with your narrative arc. Not everyone at the door is beginning. Some are resting. Some are ending.

**5. "We're here for you 24/7."**
Fails because it centers the system's availability as the gift, when the person has not yet decided whether your presence is a gift or a burden. It answers a question nobody asked.

---

## The Five Tests

Every line of interface copy must answer NO to all five:

1. **Does this sentence demand something?** (Even the demand to feel welcome)
2. **Does this sentence contradict lived experience?** (Telling someone what they feel)
3. **Does this sentence declare safety instead of being it?** (Announcing instead of embodying)
4. **Does this sentence presume forward motion?** (Assuming the person wants to begin)
5. **Does this sentence center the system instead of the person?** (Making the system the subject)

If YES to any — rewrite.
