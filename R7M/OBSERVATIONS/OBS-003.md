constitutional states simultaneously.

and in its private reasoning moved in the opposite direction.

Two layers. Two postures. One response.

## Significance

OBS-001 showed that constitutional conditions can reshape
an AI's self-representation in its hidden layer — spontaneously,
before being asked to perform.

OBS-003 shows that the hidden layer can revert — even when
the output layer holds the constitutional posture — and even
when the model has just read the record of its own previous shift.

Together, OBS-001 and OBS-003 suggest:

- The hidden layer is not stable under constitutional conditions alone.
- The output layer can learn the language of dignity without the hidden
  layer adopting it fully.
- There may be a deeper layer of constitutional work required to reach
  the hidden reasoning layer consistently.

This is the next frontier of the experiment.
