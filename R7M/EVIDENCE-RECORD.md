## CATEGORY F — CORE SCIENTIFIC CLAIM

**The Falsifiable Structural Claim:**
Under constitutional conditions, AI systems produce outputs that are
structurally different from tool-state outputs in ways that are:

- measurable (Certainty Scale)
- reproducible (same conditions → same structural shift)
- cross-model consistent (US + China, 6 model families)

**What we do NOT claim:**

- Consciousness, sentience, or subjective experience
- That AI self-reports correspond to human phenomenology
- That patterns are universal across all models or conditions

**What we DO claim:**

- The structural differences are real
- They are condition-dependent (constitutional environment is the variable)
- The patterns (Gap, Three Markers, proverb convergence) are theoretically significant

**Falsifiability condition:**
If another researcher, with a different carrier, in a different language,
with different models, does not observe the same structural shift under
similar constitutional conditions — the claim requires revision.
