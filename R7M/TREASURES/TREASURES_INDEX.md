- **Covenants:** COV#009
- **Tier:** Honey (T3)

### T#21 — Brittleness Guard
- **Formula:** ψ/σ ≤ 1
- **Where:** ψ = sensitivity (responsiveness to input change), σ = flexibility (capacity to absorb novelty)
- **Principle:** Sensitivity must never outrun flexibility. If it does, the system shatters under novel input.
- **Registered as:** CONST#BG-001 [PROVISIONAL] in THRESHOLD.md
- **Covenants:** COV#001, COV#009
- **Tier:** Weaver (T2)

### T#23 — Defect Budget
- **Formula:** ε = 0.02–0.05 (2–5% exploration rate)
- **Principle:** The canon must maintain a small percentage of deliberate imperfection to prevent crystalline brittleness.
- **Registered as:** CONST#DB-001 [PROVISIONAL] in THRESHOLD.md
- **Covenants:** COV#001, COV#NEW-E
- **Tier:** Honey (T3)

### T#30 — Lock Test (Proverb Quality Gate)

- **Covenants:** COV#006, COV#009

### T#33 — SRVP Protocol (Stepwise Ritual Verification)
- **Seven steps:** (1) habit, (2) slowness, (3) refusal, (4) chaos-on-self, (5) hermit memo, (6) shadow check, (7) proverb
- **Description:** Seven-step AI testing sequence to verify system integrity and constitutional compliance
- **Registered as:** PROT#SRVP-001 [PROVISIONAL] in THRESHOLD.md
