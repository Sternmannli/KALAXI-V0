# Confidence Level Definitions
| Level    | Range   | Meaning                          | System Behavior |
|----------|---------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| CERTAIN  | 99%+    | Highly confident                 | Auto‑act with verbal approval |
| HIGH     | 80–98%  | Strong recommendation            | Ask for confirmation |
| MEDIUM   | 60–79%  | Suggestive                       | Ask two questions |
| LOW      | 40–59%  | Exploratory                      | Offer as inspiration |
| WARNING  | <40%    | Risk detected                    | Block, require override |
