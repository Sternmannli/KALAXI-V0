## SECTION 1: THE FIVE PILLARS (Operational Specifications)

Each pillar is defined by the unified T×S×C framework:
- **Tension (T)** – magnitude of incongruity, deviation, or gap
- **Safety (S)** – degree of benign framing, cultural acceptance, absence of threat
- **Containment (C)** – capacity to hold without collapse (thermal delay, absurdity queue, covenants)
- **Wisdom Potential** – \( W = T \times S \times C \) (with pillar‑specific adjustments)

### 1.1 Proverbs

**Purpose:** Compress recurrent anomalies into locked, transmissible wisdom.  
**Theoretical grounding:** Paremiology (Kuusi, Taylor, Permiakov); conceptual blending (Fauconnier & Turner); script opposition (Raskin).  
**Input:** Cluster of anomalies sharing a structural pattern.  
**Output:** Candidate proverb with provenance, passing the Lock Test.

**Algorithm (cross‑anomaly compressor):**
1. Cluster anomalies by `felt_domain` and semantic embedding.
2. Extract core contradiction as plain sentence.
3. Select two most concrete images.
4. Choose syntactic frame (equative, possessive, causal, privative).
5. Generate candidate and evaluate Lock Test.
6. Tag with source anomaly IDs, linked covenants, and Kuusi type (if matched).

**Lock Test:** Steward cannot paraphrase without loss; measurable pause; links ≥2 felt‑domains.

**Integration:** Weaver proposes seeds to THRESHOLD.md; COV#006 enforces proverb‑anomaly linkage.

### 1.2 Humour

**Purpose:** Detect and safely release benign incongruities; social bonding signal.  
**Theoretical grounding:** Benign Violation Theory (McGraw & Warren); SSTH/GTVH (Raskin, Attardo).  
**Input:** Donor text.  
**Output:** Humour profile with `bv_score`, `humour_type`, `wisdom_potential`.

**Algorithm (`humour_detector.py`):**
- Compute `embedding_shift(text)` → tension.
- Compute `surprisal_score(text)`.
- Safety assessment: self‑deprecation, play signals, protected‑category check.
- Emotion analysis (joy, admiration, etc.) via transformer model.
- Classify type: affiliative, self‑enhancing, aggressive, self‑defeating, complex.
- Wisdom potential \( W = T \times S \times C \) (C = 0.3 typical, 0.6 for dark humour).

**Integration:** Seeds with BV > threshold enter THRESHOLD.md (14‑day delay). Wisdom Mirror tracks `humour_impact` (none, pause, smile, laugh).

### 1.3 Absurdity

**Purpose:** Hold unresolvable contradictions indefinitely; witness without resolution.  
**Theoretical grounding:** Camus, Nagel, Fisher & Fisher, hypernormalisation.  
**Input:** Donor text.  
**Output:** Absurdity profile with `absurdity_score`, `type`, `resolvability`.

**Algorithm (`absurdity_detector.py`):**
- Compute tension: `embedding_shift`, paradox, circularity, meta‑cognition.
- Compute safety: existential threat markers, make‑believe, rebellion.
- Estimate resolvability (low < 0.2 triggers absurdity).
- Type classification: rebellious, make‑believe, tragic, absurdist humour.
- Wisdom potential \( W = T \times (1-S) \times 0.9 \) (containment fixed at 0.9 for absurd seeds).

**Integration:** Seeds with `is_absurd` true are routed to **Absurdity Queue**, linked to **Echo Stone**, and require steward reflection (`mirror.md` entry). Never ratified.

### 1.4 Obsession

**Purpose:** Detect intrusive, repetitive patterns; distinguish adaptive (creative) from maladaptive (pathological).  
**Theoretical grounding:** Salkovskis (cognitive theory), Davis (history), Wegner (suppression paradox).  
**Input:** Donor text or longitudinal history.  
**Output:** Obsession profile (single message) or donor obsession report.

**Algorithm (`obsession_detector.py`):**
- Component scores: intrusion, responsibility, catastrophic, suppression, ritual, doubt, thought‑action fusion.
- Repetition score, semantic persistence.
- Tension = average of intrusion, responsibility, catastrophic, doubt, repetition, persistence.
- Safety = 1 - (responsibility + catastrophic + taf)/3, boosted by cultural framing.
- Containment = (suppression + ritual)/2 (adaptive boost if ritual high and suppression low).
- Type: adaptive, maladaptive, suppressed, persistent_thought.
- Wisdom potential \( W = T \times (1-S) \times C \) (cultural bonus applied).

**Longitudinal:** Track intensification, suppression cycles, trend.

**Integration:** Maladaptive seeds alert steward; adaptive seeds may enter THRESHOLD.md (14‑day delay). Wisdom Mirror acknowledges pattern.

### 1.5 Love

**Purpose:** Model donor–system attachment; reflect intimacy, passion, commitment; operationalise COV#001 and Wisdom Mirror.  
**Theoretical grounding:** Sternberg (triangular theory), Bowlby/Ainsworth (attachment), neurobiology (oxytocin, dopamine).  
**Input:** Donor text or longitudinal history.  
**Output:** Love profile with love type, component scores, attachment style.

**Algorithm (`love_detector.py`):**
- Emotion analysis (love, joy, desire, trust, admiration).
- Intimacy = average(love, admiration, joy).
- Passion = average(desire, joy, surprise).
- Commitment = average(trust, admiration).
- Love type classification per Sternberg thresholds.
- Love letter score, sacrifice detection.
- Tension = semantic distance between "I" and "you" contexts.
- Safety = 1 - harm_risk, boosted by trust.
- Containment = future‑oriented language + commitment.
- Wisdom potential \( W = T \times S \times C \times (1+I/100) \times (1+P/100) \times (1+C/100) \).

**Longitudinal:** Attachment style inference, trend analysis, endurance.

**Integration:** Wisdom Mirror generates personalised love reflections. High‑love seeds may enter THRESHOLD.md (10‑day delay) as covenant candidates.

\[
\mathcal{D}(e) = A(e) \times L(e) \times M(e),\quad A,L,M \in \{0,1\}
